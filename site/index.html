Virtual ONVIF / RTSP / SRT IP Camera for POS & Desktops

Ultra-lightweight Rust alternative to ITVDesk / DeskCamera. Stream desktops, webcams, microphones, and operator activity overlays directly to Hikvision, Dahua, Milestone, Frigate, and Synology.

Built for 24/7 POS Auditing & Standard VMS

POS Activity Overlays

Animated click ripples, borderless keystroke OSD with PCI digit masking, and live POS ticket text via REST & WebSocket.

Visual Privacy Masks

Draw and resize normalized blackout rectangles directly over the live Web UI preview to protect PIN pads and card fields.

ONVIF Profile S & T

WS-Discovery auto-discovery, WS-Security auth, H.264 & H.265 Media2, PullPoint Motion/POS events, and HTTP JPEG snapshots.

Webcam, Mic & Talkback

Optional webcam PiP or dedicated /webcam RTSP channel, G.711 PCMU/PCMA audio, and two-way NVR-to-POS backchannel.

Pure-Rust SRT WAN Push

Stream encrypted MPEG-TS over SRT across CG-NAT/LTE to central cloud VMS relays without external C++ libsrt deps.

Zero-Idle & Fleet CLI

Pauses encoding near 0% CPU when idle. Deploy across POS fleets via nvrdesk fleet init, Ansible, GPO PowerShell, or Snap.
